{# Dictation into a text box, shared by the items screen and the action-item update screen. One dictation at a time: Dictate.toggle({box, btn, note(text, cls), onDone(text, heard), belongs()}) starts (or stops) filling `box`; `btn` shows the mic / stop icon; note() reports progress; onDone gets the final text when it ends; belongs() says whether live words may still go into the box (false once the user has moved to another item). Dictate.stop(), Dictate.box (the box being filled, or null), Dictate.MIC (the mic icon). #}
