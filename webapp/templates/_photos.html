{# Photos for an item or an action-item update, the same on both screens. Photos.box(el, photos, {onCamera, onLibrary, onOpen}) draws the photo box: empty, a tap opens the camera, with an "or choose from library" link; with photos, the first one (whole) and a 1/3 badge, and a tap calls onOpen. Photos.open(store, at) shows the photo screen: one photo large with Retake and Delete, and Camera / Library to add more. store is {title(i, n), list() -> [{url}], add(files), retake(i, file), remove(i)} (add / retake / remove may be async), so the item screen can upload at once while the update screen keeps photos on the phone until Save. Photos.pick("camera" | "library", files => ...) opens the phone's camera (one photo) or its photo library (several). On a computer both are the file picker. #}
