{# First-time tips: a few bubbles pointing at a screen's main controls, one at a time, shown once per phone or computer. The first time the app opens in a browser, Tips.welcome(then) asks whether the user wants a tutorial. Yes turns tips on (then `then` runs the home screen's tips); No turns them off everywhere. Until then, and after a No, no screen shows tips. Tips.run("project", [{at: "#btnNewVisit", text: "..."}, ...]) shows a screen's tips when they are on and this browser has not seen that screen's yet; steps whose control is missing or hidden are skipped. The choice and the "seen" notes live in this browser's storage (localStorage), never on the server, so updating the app does not bring them back; bump VERSION[screen] to show one screen's tips once more after a big change. Tips.reset() (App settings → Show tips again) turns them back on for every screen; Tips.off() turns them off. #}
