{# First-time tips: a few bubbles pointing at a screen's main controls, one at a time, shown once per phone or computer. Tips.run("project", [{at: "#btnNewVisit", text: "..."}, ...]) shows a screen's tips unless this browser has already seen them; steps whose control is missing or hidden are skipped. The note that they were seen lives in this browser's storage (localStorage), never on the server, so updating the app does not bring them back; bump VERSION[screen] to show one screen's tips once more after a big change. Tips.reset() (App settings → Show tips again) brings them all back. #}
